# 13 November 2026 — The Day in Five Stories

An editorial front-page collage summing up the world news of Friday
13 November 2026, as reported that day. One headline banner, five
labelled panels — each panel is one of the day's biggest stories:

1. **UKRAINE TALKS** — Russia agreed to European participation in the
   next round of Ukraine peace negotiations, after US envoys Steve
   Witkoff and Jared Kushner met Vladimir Putin in Moscow; a prisoner
   exchange described as potentially the war's largest was also
   announced. (AP / Bloomberg / NPR via The Silk Paradox; Open
   Chronicle; Bangladesh Chronicle)
2. **NVIDIA CEO RESIGNS** — Nvidia founder Jensen Huang resigned as
   chief executive and from the board; CFO Colette Kress became interim
   CEO and shares fell about 16%. (Seven Lights Daily Brief;
   Open Chronicle; Bangladesh Chronicle)
3. **MICROSOFT OUTAGE** — A major Microsoft 365 outage disrupted mail
   and Teams for businesses, schools and government agencies
   worldwide. (Open Chronicle; Seven Lights Daily Brief)
4. **GAZA HOSPITAL STRIKE** — An Israeli strike on the al-Yasin
   surgical building at Nasser hospital in Khan Younis killed six
   people, including a surgeon. (Seven Lights Daily Brief)
5. **HORMUZ TANKER MINE** — Iran said an LNG tanker struck a naval
   mine in the Strait of Hormuz. (Bangladesh Chronicle)

The program copies the pre-rendered image (`art.png`) to
